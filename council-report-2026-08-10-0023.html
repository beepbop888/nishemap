Chairman's verdict

Where the council agrees

  • The backend is the product, and it doesn't exist. All five advisors converged: submissions dying in localStorage make the crowd-fed strategy fiction. Fix sized at under a day. Launching in TG meme channels before it exists burns the one viral spike you get.
  • Tier 4: yes, with a joke. Excluding шаурма makes the map "wrong about its own subject" (Outsider). The new tier must be MORE self-deprecating, not a quiet loosening.
  • OSM gray pins are bait, not inventory — a call-to-action baked into the map, never content to brag about.

Where the council clashes

  • Gray pins: alive or abandoned? Executor/Expansionist vs Outsider/Contrarian. Resolution: gray pins are poison before the submission loop works, bait after.
  • Chains: fastest coverage (Expansionist) vs off-story wallpaper (Executor). Both right — phase-3 move.
  • Approval queue vs instant visibility: the deepest split. First Principles' reframe won the reviews: Geojimap's engine is the confession act — submit → your pin is publicly visible within minutes. An admin queue is where confessions go to die.

Blind spots caught in review

  • Moderation under viral load — missed by ALL five advisors, flagged by all five reviewers. Open write path + zero moderation = trust dies from garbage data instead of vanished data. Cheap tripwires: ip-hash rate limiting, price sanity bounds, one-tap flagging into a daily-eyeballed sheet.
  • Founder bandwidth is the actual constraint — nobody budgeted one person's hours across backend/walk/moderation/launch.
  • 16/125 district coverage illusion; Supabase free-tier limits under the exact spike being optimized for; RU legal exposure of public price claims — 30 minutes of thought each before launch week.

The recommendation

1. Ship tier 4 as a separated dull-copper coin with escalated humor labeling — one config constant.

2. Population order: backend → founder walk → OSM import → TG bounties → chains.

3. Build the thinnest live backend with instant public pins + three cheap guards. Everything else is furniture.

The one thing to do first: today — Supabase project, one submissions table, wire the existing form to POST, render rows as pins. By tonight a stranger's submission should become a pin another stranger can see. Until that round trip works, don't touch tiers, OSM, or publicity.
