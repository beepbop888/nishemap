Sprites on a 24×24 grid, drawn entirely by me. The samples below are a proof of concept, not finished art.

My recommendation

B — pixel art

Not because the sample below is good. It is rough. I am recommending it because it is the only option where the failure mode you keep hitting cannot happen. Every complaint you have made — things overlaying, hats covering eyes, props sitting on top instead of being worn — comes from me placing smooth curves against a head I cannot measure precisely. On a pixel grid there is nothing to misplace: a hat occupies exact cells, and if it covers an eye I can see that cell and move it.

It is also the right genre. You are selling collectibles to people who play games on their phone. Pixel art reads as unlocked. And it survives 28 px in a leaderboard, which the illustrated styles barely do — compare the small copies above.

The trade-off I am accepting: all 20 characters get hand-drawn cell by cell, and the first two or three will be bad before the style settles. That is real work, roughly a day, and you would be approving sprites in batches rather than all at once.

If you would rather not gamble: C (Avataaars) is the safe pick — its 34 built-in hats and 9 clothing items mean most Moscow character comes from real parts instead of my drawn props. Less distinctive, far fewer ways for me to get it wrong.

How avatars and awards actually get into the app

You asked when, where, who sees it, and how it is explained. Here is the plan.

QuestionAnswer
Who sees avatarsEveryone. Website and Telegram both. Your avatar shows on your pin, in the venue card next to prices you submitted, and on the leaderboard.
Who can keep themAnyone, but only Telegram users keep them across devices. On the website the wallet lives in that browser. This is the honest reason to link Telegram, and it should be stated plainly rather than hidden.
Where you change itTap your own avatar in the header. That opens the shop — owned avatars first, then the ladder with prices, locked ones dimmed with the coin cost on them.
Where awards liveSame panel, second tab. A grid of all 8 medals, earned ones in colour with the ribbon, unearned ones greyed with the count needed. Tapping an earned one opens the share tile.
When the animation firesThe coin the moment a price is confirmed. The medal the first time you cross a threshold, once only, on next open — never mid-task.
How it is explainedThree sentences in the About panel, and one line under the submit form the first time: «Сдал цену → её подтвердили → монета. На монеты берёшь аватары.» Nobody reads more than that.
What blocks launchCoins are counted in the browser today, so they are forgeable. That is fine for avatars. It is not fine the moment coins buy anything real — that needs the server-side ledger and Telegram signature check first.

Order I would build it

  1. You pick a style here. Nothing else moves until that is settled.
  2. I draw 3 characters in it and you approve or reject the look.
  3. I finish the remaining 17 in batches.
  4. Avatar picker and awards tab go into the header panel.
  5. Telegram link, so progress survives a new phone.
  6. Server-side coin ledger — before any coupon or partner offer exists.
