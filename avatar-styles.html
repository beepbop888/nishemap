Where I stand

Pixel art, still

Row C is the most polished right now, and if you want to stop iterating today, take it. But it is polished because the library did the work, and it will always look like the library — the cosmonaut proves the ceiling. Row B is worse today and better later: it is the only row where I control every cell, and every complaint you have raised has been about placement I could not control.

What row B needs: a pass on beards, the hockey cage, and the space visor. That is a few hours, not another week.
